# QuickBite PH Food Website
## Project Overview
This food ordering website was developed by BSIS–3A students as part of their Web Development course,
showcasing the services, objectives, and features of QuickBite PH as a fictional online food delivery platform.
## Purpose
To bring fresh, flavorful meals closer to everyone by providing a fast, reliable, and user - friendly online
ordering experience that delivers high-quality food directly from QuickBite PH’s kitchen to every customer.

## Site Structure
- `index.html` – Homepage
- `menu.html` – List of menu
- `contacts.html` – contact forms
- `promotions.html` – promo deals
- `about_us.html` – our story, mission & vision, objectives
- `faq.html` – company's frequently asked questions
- `style.css` – styling and designing
- `script.js` – interactivity & enhancement
- `assets/images` – Images and icons
## Design Concept
- **Color Scheme**: Warm beige tones with high-contrast dark text for readability.
- **Typography**: Poppins for headings and Open Sans for clear body text.
- **Layout**: Clean, grid-based desktop layout with clearly separated sections.
